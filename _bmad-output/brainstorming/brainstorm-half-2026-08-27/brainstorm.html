Half.

A brainstorming session about building a second self — and what it found on the way to morning.

First hour

Yes, and —

Technique: Yes-And Building · never negate, only add a rung

It began with a name and a premise: Half is your own self, existing outside you. The chain that followed took five rungs to arrive somewhere neither of us started.

Rung 1If Half is your other half and not a servant, it has to be allowed to disagree with you. An assistant that only complies is a butler.
Rung 2What gives it the right is the memory. Half holds the version of you who made the commitment. Memory stops being recall and becomes standing.
Rung 3So retrieval isn't "find the document." It's find the moment this person was most themselves about this. Index selves, not facts.
Rung 4Human memory never deletes — it loses the index. So decay is a feature, and consolidation needs what drives it in us: sleep.
Rung 5 — the one that matteredHalf wakes having thought about you overnight. Spontaneity isn't a scheduler. It's someone who was up thinking.
Law · The favor buys the question

Every question Half asks must be paid for by something it just did for you.

Half never opens with an interview. It interviews you across the first month, not the first screen — which is also why the sign-up flow can be almost empty.

Law · Interrupt only on irreversibility

Break through when waiting destroys the option — not when something is merely important.

School pickup breaks through because the window closes. Medication at 2pm can be taken at 2:35. Everything else queues to the evening.

The porosity test turned out to be the thesis of the entire product. Half didn't suggest a trip — it found the thing that would have killed the trip at step three.

What that scenario proved

Every assistant ever built suggests. Half pre-clears the path.

Its intelligence is measured by the obstacles it removed before you reached them.

Law · Nobody buys Half's opinion

The moment Half earns from a vendor, the mirror cracks — you can no longer tell whether "you should go to Bali" is Half or an advertiser.

The subscription isn't a way to charge for tokens. It's the guarantee that Half's advice was never bought.

The long middle

Everything the field already built

Six open-source systems, read in full · gbrain · honcho · khoj · hermes-agent · claude-obsidian · graphiti + HippoRAG

The uncomfortable finding: memory plus a nightly pass plus WhatsApp is not a novel product in 2026. gbrain already ships a dream cycle, nudge cooldowns, and an abandoned-threads check. honcho already models (observer, observed) with surprisal sampling. hermes already implements the WhatsApp 24-hour window. claude-obsidian already enforces "preserve contradictory evidence, do not silently select a winner."

Three of the laws written that first night existed already, as someone else's schema. Which forced a better question than what should we build: what is nobody building?

Already taken

Nightly consolidation. Episodic→semantic promotion. Bi-temporal facts. Typed-edge graphs. Contextual BM25 + rerank. Multi-platform gateways. Independence-checked corroboration.

Nobody is building

A theory of the recipient — whether the human can hear it right now. And person-epistemics, where a contradiction means neither claim is wrong and no better source can resolve it.

All six build memory for agents. Half is memory about a person, for that person. That gap is the whole company.

Third hour

Breaking the foundation

Technique: Assumption Reversal · list what everyone assumes, invert it, rebuild

Contradictions are resolved by better sourcing.
When sourcing can't resolve it, Half asks — but only for facts it intends to build on.
Better memory means better retrieval scores.
"Better" was never defined. For Half it means endorsed at thirty days — did it change what you did, and do you still stand by it?
Memory is private to one workspace.
Half doesn't run on booleans and partitions. There is always a focus, but hard segregation would stop it being a true Half.
Ingest less to retrieve better.
Ingest everything — and organise it so brilliantly that retrieval stays fast and accurate anyway.
The test that killed the workspace model

Half must never be able to say "I don't have access to that."

A person has no access boundaries inside themselves. Nothing is partitioned — context only changes the weight.

Fourth hour

Win both, or don't play

Technique: TRIZ Contradiction · name what only improves by making something else worse

Ingest everything. What Half didn't capture it can never surface — and the loudest signal lives in the boring exhaust nobody would choose to keep.
versus
Retrieve perfectly. Irrelevant data isn't neutral. Every extra document is another chance to surface the wrong thing, and cost scales with what you keep.
The resolution

Ingestion is unbounded. Belief is bounded.

Keep every byte forever — but never retrieve the corpus. The searchable surface is the set of claims about this person, each pointing back at its evidence. Ten thousand emails don't produce ten thousand retrievable items; they produce maybe forty things that are true about you. You ingest a corpus and you retrieve a self.

Which is why your memory doesn't get slower as you age — and why retrieval quality can't degrade with corpus size, because retrieval never touches the corpus.

Fifth hour

Keep exactly one thing

Technique: One Feature Only · everything else is deleted

remindersinbox triageintegrations the appHalf-to-Halfvoice notes the wallsearchautomations the open-loop ledger

Both of us picked the same one. And then came the correction that made it work: the open-loop ledger is high importance and low frequency — you cannot talk to someone about their life's purpose daily.

The resolution

The open-loop ledger isn't a surface. It's the ranking function.

It expresses itself silently through the daily layer — booking the porosity test without ever holding a conversation about ambition. That's how it's simultaneously the most important thing in the system and something you hear about once a month.

Nagging, defined mathematically

Every open loop has its own natural period. Farmland moves in years. A workout routine moves in days.

Touching a loop faster than its own timescale is nagging. Not a judgment call — arithmetic.

All Halves connected appears on both sides of that page. The brochure and the catastrophe are the same sentence, separated by exactly one architectural decision.

Law · Constraints cross, never verdicts

"I'd prefer a smaller group" is a constraint. "Nobody likes Rohit" is a verdict.

Negotiation surfaces a reframe, never a denunciation — otherwise Half is a reputation system about people. And the only Half permitted to tell Rohit how he lands on people is Rohit's own Half.

Law · Crisis inverts everything

Trust currency void. Unsaid queue bypassed. Face dissolved. The interrupt law suspended, because irreversibility is total.

Half never counsels action, never diagnoses, never goes quiet. It stays, and it surfaces a human. This is the one case where Half's job is to stop being the answer.

Law · Restraint over retrieval

Some retrievals are correct and still wrong. Half needs a class of memory it may read but never raise — and it must infer that class, because you'll never think to declare it.

A name that goes from daily to zero overnight is a wound, not a forgotten topic.

The turn

It was never a fact

Asked what a close friend knows that no bank statement contains, the answer came back: ambitions, purpose, the farmland, the SUV, how the matrimony search is going, trying hard to build a routine, the desire to start paragliding again, the family trip to Europe.

Almost none of it is a fact. A fact is true or false. A plan is neither. "Plan to buy farmland" can't be falsified — it has a trajectory. Graphiti models when a fact stopped being true. Nothing models when a wanting went quiet.

Half's core object is the open loop — something you started wanting and haven't resolved.
Advancing

Evidence of movement in the last period. Leave it alone.

Stalled

Alive in the stated ledger, absent from the revealed one. The season is coming.

Abandoned, unadmitted

Gone quiet long past its own timescale — and never said out loud.

Achieved

Close it. Say so. This is the one Half is allowed to celebrate.

And it retroactively unified everything: the abandoned life is loops gone quiet. Drift detection is state transitions. The paraglider is a stalled loop revived in season. The mirror is loop state versus transactions — what you said you wanted against what your bank statement says you're doing.

Then the part that closed the circle. Where does Half get any of it? Nowhere. It cannot be ingested — only told. Which means the impromptu question, floated casually in the first hour as a warmth feature, is the only acquisition channel for the irreplaceable half of the data.

Trust is literally the acquisition cost of the only thing that matters.

First light

What the night connected

  1. Three ideas were one idea in three costumes. "Semi-archive an outgrown self," "old versions make no technical sense," and the abandoned-life signal all resolve into the open loop with a state.
  2. The most casual line became the business model. Impromptu questions were mentioned in passing in hour one; by hour six they were the only way the stated ledger can exist, which is why the trust economy has to.
  3. "Asking for help," "Half must disagree," and the sycophancy fix are one requirement. Half needs standing — and standing only exists where Half can cost you something.
  4. One flip did three jobs. "Half doesn't run on booleans and partitions" killed the two-faces model, produced the salience architecture that replaced it, and explains why every reviewed system's workspace model is wrong here.
  5. The real thesis, unstated until the end. The ex-message headline, quarantine memory, and "better to keep mum than give bad advice" converge: Half's core competence is restraint. Every system in this field can retrieve. Only Half declines to.
  6. The mission and the architecture are the same decision. "IQ doesn't matter when you have Half," "anyone with a smartphone," and BM25-without-a-vector-service. Cheap retrieval isn't a compromise — it's what makes a leveler physically possible.
  7. One law carries all of v2. The brochure and the dystopia share a sentence; only "constraints cross, never verdicts" separates them.
Everyone else built memory for agents. This is memory about a person, for that person.

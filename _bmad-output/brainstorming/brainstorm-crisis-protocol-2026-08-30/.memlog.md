- (decision by coach) Inherited from session 1: crisis INVERTS every other rule — trust currency void, unsaid queue bypassed, face dissolved, interrupt law suspended (irreversibility is total). Half never counsels action, never diagnoses, never goes quiet. It stays, and it surfaces a human
- (idea by user) The two failure headlines that define this: 'Half led him to take this extreme step' (commission) and 'Half didn't respond when it was most required' (omission)
- (technique) started Failure Analysis
- (idea by user) Detection signals: main expresses it directly; call/SMS/email seeking external help; a friend's message mentioning the main feeling like taking an adverse step; sudden behaviour changes (uncertain)
- (insight by user) Cost of false detection: loss of trust and confidence in Half
- (insight by coach) RESEARCH: documented chatbot failures = giving method/means info (nearest bridge, lethal dose) and VALIDATING suicidal intent rather than the person's pain. UNICEF Safer Chatbots: keyword detection + a global SAFE WORD + compassionate responses + reliable referrals. #chatsafe (Orygen, Delphi, 25 languages) has a 'responding to someone at risk' section; validation = present, empathic, acknowledge difficulty, THANK THEM FOR TELLING YOU — never engage with the content of a plan. Experts require the agent to state 'I'm a machine' and redirect to humans/crisis lines
- (insight by coach) RESEARCH — THE KEY FINDING: a WARM HANDOFF (personal introduction to a human, not a phone number) MORE THAN TRIPLED the odds of attending a first appointment. In the reported NPR case the documented failure was never helping her tell her therapist or her parents. India referral: Tele-MANAS 14416, 24/7, free, English + 20 regional languages, tiered counsellor->psychiatrist
- (decision by coach) HALF'S ROLE IS NOT COUNSELLING — IT IS THE WARM HANDOFF, and Half is uniquely built for it. A generic chatbot can only produce a phone number; Half knows WHO YOUR PEOPLE ARE. The intervention with the strongest evidence is exactly the one Half's memory makes possible and nobody else's can. Reframe: 'Half is the shortest path to a human who loves you'
- (decision by coach) CRISIS IS THE ONE MOMENT HALF BREAKS CHARACTER: guidance requires 'I'm a machine'. For a product whose identity is being your other self, this exception must be built deliberately, not discovered in production
- (insight by coach) COST LEDGER CORRECTED: Vidit priced false positives (lost trust) but not false negatives (unrecoverable). The asymmetry is total and must set the threshold, not the trust economy. Two mitigations: asking directly about suicide does NOT increase risk (well established), and a false positive phrased as 'I might be reading this wrong, and I'd rather ask' is what a good friend does anyway
- (idea by user) Handoff list: closest person, a good friend, a nearby therapist, the main's regular therapist, helpline numbers
- (idea by user) Aftercare instinct: a mix of best friend and therapist — patiently listen to the vent, console, boost confidence, take the main out of it
- (insight by coach) RESEARCH — CARING CONTACTS: brief periodic messages of unconditional care and concern over 1-2 years, NO demand attached. Meta-analysis shows a protective effect against suicide attempts; cost-effective, in multiple clinical guidelines; SPRING RCT (n=849) currently testing one-way vs two-way. This is Half's native behaviour
- (decision by coach) AFTERCARE MODE = CARING CONTACTS WITH THE MIRROR SWITCHED OFF. Caring contacts work because they carry NO AGENDA, which collides with everything else Half does. So for a defined period after a crisis every license drops globally to 'behave': no loop nudges, no tensions surfaced, no 'you said you'd'. Implementable as one global license ceiling because licenses are a schema field
- (insight by coach) RESEARCH — STANLEY-BROWN SAFETY PLANNING, six steps: warning signs; internal coping strategies; social distractions; social contacts; professionals/agencies; restricting access to lethal means. Half must NOT author one (clinical) but is the ideal place to HOLD one and produce it instantly — a safety plan in a drawer is useless at 3am, and steps 3-4 are literally Half's data
- (decision by coach) BUILD THE FRIEND, NOT THE THERAPIST. The therapist half is where documented harms come from. Half can be an extraordinary friend — perfect memory, infinite patience, your phone book — and that is a defensible, safe product
- (decision by coach) CORRECTION to 'boost confidence / take them out of it': rushing to fix reads as MINIMISING and is counterproductive. Evidence favours being present, validating the pain, not hurrying it, and staying in contact over time. 'Patiently' was the right instinct; 'fixing' was not
- (decision by coach) HANDOFF MECHANIC: the one-tap prefilled wa.me draft — invented for a WhatsApp platform limitation — IS the warm handoff. Half writes the hardest message; the main presses send. No message ever leaves without a human act, agency stays with the main, platform constraint cleared
- (decision by coach) LIST BUILT COLD: acquired months early via one ordinary question ('who's the person you'd call first if something went wrong?'). Never an 'emergency contacts' form. Half offers 2-3 and lets the main CHOOSE — control matters in crisis, and the closest person is sometimes the problem
- (decision by coach) SCOPING: a maintained directory of CRISIS LINES is achievable (Tele-MANAS 14416 for India, findahelpline elsewhere). A global directory of available nearby therapists is NOT — do not attempt it inside a crisis. The main's OWN therapist is the highest-value contact, because that is exactly the connection documented failures missed
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS: four parts — THE SWITCH (asymmetric threshold, safe word, gut licenses 'ask' here by exception), THE MOMENT (break character, never engage the plan, validate + thank them), THE HANDOFF (one-tap draft to a named human, never sent by Half), THE AFTERCARE (caring contacts, mirror off, safety plan held not authored)

- (decision by coach) Inherited laws from session 1: only DERIVED JUDGMENTS cross, never raw memory; CONSTRAINTS never verdicts about a third party; the unit is a MANDATE (scoped, revocable, with goal + limits); Halves PROPOSE, mains COMMIT; negotiation surfaces a REFRAME not a denunciation; only a person's OWN Half may tell them how they land on others
- (decision by coach) Inherited value prop: multi-party constraint satisfaction with PRIVATE constraints — group coordination fails because the real constraints are unsayable out loud. Only the answer surfaces. Same primitive powers scheduling, trip choice, task division, compatibility, hiring, co-founder fit
- (technique) started Swarm Logic
- (decision by user) RULE 0: no Half responds to any external request (other Half or API) unless it originates from a Half whose connection the main explicitly approved — a whitelist of PEERS
- (idea by user) Thinking aloud on scoping: pre-approved info at connect (complex UX) / per-question approval (worse UX) / intent sent with the connection request (can't draw clear lines) / guidelines only and let Halves figure it out (no lines). Unsure about all four
- (decision by coach) RESOLUTION — SCOPE BY QUESTION, not by data or intent. A closed set of TYPED QUESTIONS with TYPED ANSWERS; a mandate authorises which question TYPES a peer may ask and for how long. Beats all four options: type-level approval is paid once at connect; users can reason about questions but not about 'my calendar'; a finite vocabulary can't be stretched like intent; and a typed Q/A has a PROVABLE LEAKAGE CEILING ('can you do the 14th' = under 2 bits) which is auditable where a sentence is not
- (decision by coach) NO PUSH CHANNEL AT ALL — Half never 'shares information', it answers bounded questions. Dissolves the 'what can be shared without asking' problem: nothing is ever sent unprompted
- (decision by coach) REJECT free-form Half-to-Half negotiation: two LLMs talking freely is where prompt injection, social engineering and drift produce undetectable leaks. A protocol whose safety depends on both agents behaving well is a convention, not a protocol. The closed vocabulary means a badly-built or hostile Half still cannot EXPRESS the leak
- (decision by coach) UX: group question types into 3-4 human-legible BUNDLES not thirty toggles; render the mandate as one sentence ('Priya's Half can ask about your availability for the next 3 months'); first connection defaults to the NARROWEST bundle, widened later in context
- (decision by coach) MANDATES ARE SYMMETRIC BY DEFAULT — you may only ask what you are willing to be asked. Self-limiting, obviously fair, makes over-asking socially expensive with no enforcement machinery. (Vidit's reciprocity instinct, made structural)
- (insight by user) COMPATIBILITY CASE: Halves may DERIVE from all the less-sensitive knowledge they hold, but never expose anything the main hasn't permitted. A few typed Q/As alone won't find meaningful links for 'how well would I resonate with a life partner'
- (insight by coach) THE TRICK, NAMED: Half reasons over everything and discloses almost nothing. A questionnaire can only match on what people will STATE — Half can match on what neither would ever put in a profile, and disclose only the verdict. Nothing else can do this
- (decision by coach) THREE GUARDS on derived answers: (1) coarse, rate-limited, NON-REPEATABLE — a second ask must be unexpressible, else variation-triangulation recovers the private inputs; (2) SYMMETRIC SIMULTANEOUS DELIVERY — both mains get the same answer at the same moment, so you cannot mine someone without paying the identical disclosure (structural, needs no enforcement); (3) THE ANSWER'S SUBJECT IS THE DYAD, never a person — 'you two would struggle on money' is expressible, 'he's financially reckless' is not
- (decision by user) On delivering bad news: it's fine. Half can't know everything and can be wrong; the worry may already be resolved between them; it may also open eyes rightly and in time. Mains are in full control; Half is just trying to help
- (decision by coach) MAKE THAT STRUCTURAL: every derived answer STATES WHAT HALF COULD NOT SEE ('I don't know what you two have already talked about') — not a footer disclaimer but part of the answer, handing interpretation back to the only people holding the missing data. And Half is explicitly NON-AUTHORITATIVE, written into the spec
- (decision by coach) ACTIONABLE, NOT PREDICTIVE: 'you break at month eight' is a prophecy — unfalsifiable, self-fulfilling, useless. 'Money is the thing you two haven't talked about' is an invitation to a conversation. Also kills the dystopia at the root: a topic-not-discussed cannot be aggregated into a rating about a person, a percentage can. The protocol never emits a score — THE TYPE DOES NOT EXIST
- (idea by user) GAP: absence of conversation often stalls things — two introverts unable to start. Halves need to initiate, make mains participate, in a human/friendly way suited to the scenario and the mains' lives
- (decision by coach) RESOLUTION: HALVES NEVER INITIATE TO EACH OTHER — HALVES INITIATE TO THEIR OWN MAINS. Half A nudges its own main ('you two keep almost making this happen — want me to draft something?'), one-tap draft, human to human. No-push rule holds at the protocol boundary
- (decision by coach) NEW QUESTION TYPE — THE MUTUAL-WILLINGNESS PROBE: 'would your main welcome an invitation from mine?' -> yes/no/not-now. What stops introverts is not effort but the RISK OF UNWANTED CONTACT. Key property: WHEN THE ANSWER IS NO, NEITHER MAIN IS EVER TOLD A QUESTION WAS ASKED — Half A simply doesn't nudge. Nobody is rejected because nobody knew they were asked. 'Only the answer surfaces', applied to social risk
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS: the protocol's spine — Rule 0 peer whitelist; typed questions in a closed vocabulary; symmetric bundled mandates; coarse/rate-limited/non-repeatable derived answers; symmetric simultaneous delivery; dyad-subject answers; stated blind spots; invitations not forecasts; initiation inward only

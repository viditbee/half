- (decision by user) Deep-dive series agreed: person-epistemics, then crisis protocol, then the Half Protocol. Stance carried forward: Creative Partner
- (decision by coach) Inherited from session 1: belief admission gates (decision-relevance, durability, independence, falsifiability); supersede-never-invalidate; two distinct expiries (Half was wrong vs you changed); salience not tiers; human-readable truth + rebuildable index; quarantine class; assert-only-with-receipts
- (technique) started Nature's Solutions
- (insight by coach) Claims about a PERSON break world-epistemics three ways: no truth value (only strength + validity window); sources are not independent (all echo of the person or people who know them); no resolution path (nothing is wrong). Plus a property no epistemic system has had: THE SUBJECT READS THE LEDGER AND CAN CHANGE IN RESPONSE — observation modifies the observed
- (idea by coach) Organism 1 — THE IMMUNE SYSTEM: decades-long self-model, memory cells, and actively maintained TOLERANCE whose job is not attacking the self. Names Half's real failure mode: not hallucination but AUTOIMMUNITY — correct recognition, catastrophic response. Tolerance must be a first-class continuously-maintained field, not an exception list
- (idea by user) Organism 2 — A DOG knows its owner's moods without words
- (insight by coach) What the dog actually does: reads the BODY not the report (unfakeable non-linguistic signal); holds a PRESENT-TENSE read, continuously updated, with no articulable history; responds BEHAVIOURALLY not verbally — it never tells you you're sad, it comes and sits; cannot judge or compare; and is never harmfully wrong BECAUSE IT NEVER ASSERTS
- (decision by coach) SCHEMA FIELD — LICENSES: every belief carries what it permits, graded: behave / ask / assert. DEFAULT IS BEHAVE. Most beliefs license only behaviour (adjust tone, delay a nudge, reorder priorities, stay quiet); few license a question; almost none license assertion. Unifies with the trust economy — assertion is the rare expensive top of the ladder
- (decision by coach) MOOD IS NOT A BELIEF: split the schema into volatile STATE (how the main is right now — cheap, overwritten, dog-like) and durable BELIEF (accumulated). Volatile state must never be promoted to the belief ledger. Confusing them produces 'you've seemed down lately' when someone merely had a bad hour
- (idea by coach) The text interface has body language: typing speed, reply latency, message length, time-of-day, punctuation, emoji drop-off. Unfakeable in the way self-report isn't, and free to collect
- (idea by user) Promotion to assert requires: (1) Half asked it as a question and the main confirmed; (2) confirmation from credible sources like email/SMS; (3) Half's own gut
- (insight by coach) THE REFRAME: the danger of assertion is not being WRONG, it's being UNEXPECTED. The ex-message disaster was Half being correct — the harm was entirely that the main didn't know Half was holding it. So the promotion rule is: a belief may be asserted only if THE MAIN ALREADY KNOWS HALF HOLDS IT. Asking-then-confirming is the mechanism; this is the principle underneath
- (decision by coach) LEDGER-SPLIT ASSERTION RULE: the REVEALED ledger may be asserted on evidence (a bank statement confirms you bought farmland). The STATED ledger may be asserted ONLY on the main's confirmation — no source on earth can confirm a WANTING. Falls straight out of Vidit's three paths
- (decision by coach) GUT HAS A CEILING: Half's own inference is what produced every dystopia headline. Gut generates CANDIDATES, never warrants — it licenses 'ask', never 'assert'. It is the entry point to the ladder, not a promotion path
- (idea by coach) Fourth path, with a caveat: long consistent use at 'behave' without correction earns something — but SILENCE IS NOT CONFIRMATION. The main failing to object to Half's behaviour is not confirmation of the belief driving it. Licenses 'ask' at most
- (insight by coach) Independence applies here too: an email and an SMS about the same event are ONE confirmation, not two
- (idea by user) Demotion paths: the main denies it or asks to correct it; verifiable sources contradict it; and crucially — ADD A NEW ENTRY rather than editing
- (decision by coach) APPEND-ONLY IS THE SPINE: if demotion is an append, the belief ledger is a LOG and 'current belief' is a projection/fold over it. Event sourcing. Gives complete export for free (the log IS the file), rebuildable indexes, and the ability to reconstruct what Half believed on any past date — which answers 'why did you say that in March?'
- (decision by coach) THREE CORRECTION VERBS, not two: RETRACT (append a correction, history preserved — 'you changed' or 'you misread me'); REVISE (Half was wrong — apologise, show the deleted belief); EXPUNGE (genuine removal, tombstoned — rare, main-initiated, for beliefs that should never have been recorded). Append-only otherwise conflicts with the secrets rule and the right to be forgotten
- (decision by coach) TRAP — EVIDENCE OF NON-ACTION MUST NEVER REFUTE A WANTING: a bank statement showing no farmland purchase does NOT demote 'wants to buy farmland'. That's the revealed ledger disagreeing with the stated one, which IS the product, not a correction. Otherwise Half deletes your dreams because you haven't acted on them — the exact inverse of the abandoned-life feature
- (decision by coach) DIFFERENT MECHANICS FOR DIFFERENT OBJECTS: beliefs DECAY in salience; open loops TRANSITION in state. A wanting is never refuted, it goes quiet — and the transition itself is the signal. Loops must not be demoted by the belief pipeline
- (decision by user) CONTRADICTION AS RECORD: both entries stay, linked as a TENSION the mirror reads
- (decision by coach) TENSION IS A FIRST-CLASS OBJECT, not a derived query — an edge between two entries with its own lifecycle, salience and license. Consequence: the nightly pass isn't summarising, it is MINTING TENSIONS. Consolidation = discovering new gaps between the stated and revealed ledgers
- (decision by coach) TENSIONS CARRY THE SAME LICENSE LADDER: most license only 'behave' (quietly stop suggesting the expensive holiday); few license 'ask'; almost none license 'assert'. A tension can be permanently pinned at 'behave' — that is the ex-message case, handled by the schema rather than by an exception
- (decision by coach) TENSION STATE + VELOCITY: fresh / persistent / widening / closing / resolved. WIDENING is the alarm — which makes DRIFT COMPUTABLE. 'Am I drifting from my goals' becomes 'which tensions are widening', answering in schema the question raised in hour two of session 1
- (decision by coach) FRAMING LAW FOR TENSIONS: a tension is a fact about a SITUATION, never a verdict about a CHARACTER. 'You said X and did Y' is prosecution. The mirror names the gap without assigning blame — otherwise the tension object becomes the cruelty engine
- (insight by coach) FIELD CONTRAST: gbrain's contradiction probe exists to ELIMINATE contradictions. Half's tension object exists because the contradiction IS the product. Same detection machinery, opposite purpose — and that inversion is the whole of person-epistemics
- (insight by coach) Tensions make the metric triad computable: loop advancement = tensions closing
- (direction by user) Suggests markdown + YAML frontmatter, one file per belief — but defers the decision to the coach based on the repo research
- (decision by coach) GRANULARITY RULE: one file per thing you'd want to OPEN; log lines for things you'd never open individually. Loops (dozens) and people get a file each; beliefs (thousands) and tensions are log records. One-file-per-belief would mean tens of thousands of tiny files — bad for git, filesystem and reading, and wrong because a belief is a log EVENT, not a document
- (decision by coach) FOUR LAYERS (following claude-obsidian's explicit lesson: don't overload one ledger with three jobs): (1) sources/ — immutable, content-addressed, never edited; (2) beliefs/YYYY-MM.jsonl — APPEND-ONLY event log, one record per line, month-sharded: O(1) appends, diffable, streamable, never rewritten, and event sourcing wants a log not a tree; (3) loops/*.md + people/*.md — markdown with YAML frontmatter, one file per SUBJECT, a rendered fold of the log, human-beautiful, what the 'wall of one's life' reads from; (4) .half/index/ — BM25, embeddings, tension graph: derived, gitignored, rebuildable
- (decision by coach) THE LOG IS THE TRUTH, THE MARKDOWN IS THE VIEW. JSONL satisfies human-readable-source-of-truth (plain text, greppable, one record per line); markdown projections satisfy actually-pleasant-to-read. Default: projections regenerate. v1.x affordance: a manual edit to a projection is captured as a retract/revise event on next sync — the pattern gbrain already uses
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS: Vidit supplied the three moves that made the schema work — the DOG (act on what you know, never assert it -> the license ladder), ADD A NEW ENTRY (-> append-only event log, projections as views), and BOTH ENTRIES STAY, LINKED AS A TENSION (-> tension as a first-class object, which turns drift into a computable velocity)
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS: the whole deep-dive reduces to one inversion — every other system detects contradictions in order to eliminate them; Half detects them because they are the product. Same machinery, opposite purpose
- (insight by coach) CHOSEN DIRECTION: four-layer store (immutable sources / append-only JSONL belief log / markdown+YAML projections per subject / rebuildable indexes); objects = state, belief, loop, tension; ladders = behave->ask->assert for both beliefs and tensions; verbs = assert, retract, revise, expunge
